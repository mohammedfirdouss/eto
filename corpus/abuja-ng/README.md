## Why Abuja is the natural second pack

Same structure as Lagos: an old law in force, loudly reported reform in progress, and
a gap between them that tenants act on at their own cost.

## Primary sources identified (checklist step 1 — not yet acquired/hashed)

- **Law in force:** Recovery of Premises Act, Cap. 544, Laws of the Federation of
  Nigeria (Abuja) — governs tenancy recovery in the FCT. Full-text PDF located at
  SabiLaw (sabilaw.org, watermarked copy of the 1945 Act as applicable to Abuja);
  a gazette-grade copy should be sourced from an official repository before use.
  Notice provisions: ss.7–8 (notice to quit; length of notice).
- **Reform in progress:** FCT tenancy reform proposals are recurringly reported;
  the current bill text and its legislative stage must be located at source
  (National Assembly / FCT authorities) — step 1 for the bill has not been done.
